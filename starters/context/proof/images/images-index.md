Your own photos beat stock every time, and they are the one thing a competitor cannot copy. Three carry most of a month:

- **Your van, storefront or office** - proves you are real
- **One before and after** from a recent job
- **You or the team** - a face beats a logo

Phone quality is fine. Better than fine, actually - polished stock reads as fake.

Once there are photos here, each one gets a line: what it shows, where it is used, and the file.

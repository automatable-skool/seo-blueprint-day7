**Warning - demo data.** Plumbera is an illustrative demo company. There is one audience here and no others.

## Who they are

**Snapshot**
A GTA homeowner, or a local business owner, with a plumbing problem. Not a plumbing expert. Values trust and a fair, clear price.

**Where they are now**
Facing something urgent or annoying - a clog, a leak, a bad smell, or no hot water - or planning an upgrade.

**Where they want to be**
Problem fixed fast, by someone they trust, with no surprise bill.

## What is bothering them

- Emergencies at the worst possible time - the holiday burst pipe, the 2am drip.
- Fear of being overcharged, upsold, or hit with a fee they never agreed to.
- Not knowing whether to DIY or call a pro, and not knowing what a job *should* cost.
- The dread of getting a quote, and the suspicion that every plumber is winging the number.

## What they want instead

Speed - same-day and 24/7 service. Transparency - upfront, written pricing. Trust - licensed, insured, background-checked, years in business, real reviews.

## What stops them calling

- "Will this cost more than quoted?"
- "Can I trust this company in my home?"
- "Is this even worth calling someone for, or can I fix it myself?"

## Which post to write for which reader

**Problem-aware** - a clog or leak right now
Emergency posts, how-to-fix posts, and "should I call a pro" posts.

**Solution-aware** - comparing options and cost
Pricing-transparency posts and service-comparison posts.

**Most-aware** - ready to book
Local trust, guarantee and "book now" posts.

## The words they actually type

"clogged drain" · "no hot water" · "leaking" · "smell coming from the drain" · "emergency plumber near me" · "how much does it cost to..." · "is this a fair price" · "plumber Toronto"

## What makes them call today

A burst pipe, a backed-up drain, a water heater failure, a renovation, a bad smell that will not go away, or a flooded basement.

## Why humour works on this reader

They are stressed, a little defensive, and braced to be sold to. A genuinely funny, honest post disarms all three at once. It signals we are human, we have seen their exact disaster, and we are not here to fleece them. Make them smile, then give them the straight answer.

## How to use a story

Match the tags to the topic. Lead with the hook, which is usually the funny or relatable part. Deliver the turning point. End on the lesson. Keep the humour PG-13 and at the situation's expense, never the customer's.

## The story bank

### The Scarborough sewer main that gave up

**Tags** · sewer line · emergency · warranty · big repair · pricing

**Hook** - A homeowner's clay sewer main collapsed, about the worst surprise a house can hand you.

**Setup** - Decades-old clay pipe finally crumbled. Full backup, no drainage.

**The problem** - The kind of job that makes a quote scary, and where shady outfits pile on "while we're in there" surprises.

**Turning point** - The crew camera-diagnosed it, quoted one upfront number, and replaced the main the same day.

**Outcome** - Full flow restored in under a day, backed by a 25-year warranty, with no midnight-rate surprise.

**Lesson** - Even a worst-case job can be a fixed, written price you agreed to first. The fear is the surprise, not the number.

**Best line** - "Quoted up front, warrantied 25 years, no magical fee that appeared at the end."

### The holiday burst pipe in Ajax

**Tags** · emergency · after-hours · winter · water damage · pricing

**Hook** - A full-house freeze during a cold snap, water everywhere it shouldn't be.

**Setup** - A pipe burst during a deep freeze, leaving the homeowner staring at a basement water feature nobody ordered.

**The problem** - Emergency, after hours, the exact moment people fear getting gouged.

**Turning point** - Crew on site fast, stopped the flood, restored water within 2 days, no after-hours surcharge.

**Outcome** - Damage contained and the bill matched the quote.

**Lesson** - When water is actively redecorating your home, calling fast is cheaper than the drywall you'd lose. A fair company doesn't double the price because it's a holiday.

**Best line** - "You're not paying for the leak. You're paying for someone to make it stop tonight."

### The condo towers that stopped flooding

**Tags** · backwater valve · prevention · commercial · drains

**Hook** - Three condo towers, a flooding problem every winter, and a fix that ended it.

**Setup** - Recurring basement flood claims across a 3-tower portfolio.

**Turning point** - Installed backwater valves across the portfolio.

**Outcome** - Flood claims cut to zero over two winters.

**Lesson** - The cheapest plumbing bill is the flood that never happens. Prevention beats emergency every time.

**Best line** - "Zero claims, two winters running."

### The $4 part and the $4,000 ceiling

**Tags** · small leak · prevention · DIY versus pro · cost

**Hook** - The cheapest part in the truck quietly destroyed a ceiling.

**Setup** - A slow drip from a worn washer, ignored for months.

**The problem** - "It's just a drip" turns into water damage downstairs.

**Turning point** - A repair that would have been $200 became a drywall, paint and insurance ordeal.

**Lesson** - Small leaks aren't small bills on a delay. Fix them early.

**Best line** - "A $4 washer and a $4,000 ceiling, connected by the same drip you've been ignoring."

### The grease that beat a restaurant, until it didn't

**Tags** · drain cleaning · commercial · relining · maintenance

**Hook** - An 8-location restaurant group kept losing the fight with its own drains.

**Setup** - A grease-blocked main causing recurring backups across sites.

**Turning point** - Cleared and relined the main, then set up a maintenance rhythm.

**Outcome** - Zero backups over six months.

**Lesson** - Drains don't get better on their own. A plan beats a panic call.

**Best line** - "Grease always wins eventually, unless someone shows up first."

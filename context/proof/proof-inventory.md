# Proof Inventory

Every claim, number, credential and trust signal allowed to appear on a page. Filled by `/context-layer`. **If it's not in this file, it doesn't go on the website.**

Three rules:

1. Never use a number or claim on a page unless it's here.
2. Never use anything from the NEVER SAY list, anywhere.
3. Never write a page for a service or area that isn't in `context/business.md`.

**Every slot below is either FOUND, MISSING or NONE.** A slot left blank with no marker means nobody looked. `MISSING` means it probably exists and hasn't been collected yet - that's the ask list. `NONE` means it genuinely doesn't exist, which is a real answer and stops it being asked again.
